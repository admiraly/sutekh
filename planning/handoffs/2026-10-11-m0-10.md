## Deviations from the task wording (recorded, not hidden)
- Harness flag is `--report-dir`, not `--report`.
- The release profile could not be built with `tools/dev.py`. The release-waiver numbers come from a scratch build with one warning class waived; they are labelled as such.
- Warm sessions restart every 15 submissions because candidate slots (16) are never evicted; the worker is warm for the timed edits of each epoch.
- Warm v2 capsule is `tests/fixtures/edit/movement_v2.capsule.json` because `examples/` has no v2 capsule (only `movement_v2.lir.json`). v1 is `examples/movement.capsule.json`.
- Tick-boundary wait is reported as unavailable (`candidate.install` is synchronous); the install RPC is reported instead.
- 1,000,000 rows was requested. The engine refuses it; the largest size that loads (250,000) was run in addition.

## Blockers and owner decisions
1. Release build fails under `-O3 -Werror`: `src/runtime/reload.c:90` (`snprintf` 255-byte reason into 160-byte buffer) and `tests/control/test_control.c:300-301`. Owner: M0-07 (reload.c) and M0-05 (test_control.c). Needs a source fix and a re-run of `--profile-label release`.
2. Worlds larger than 8 MiB of fixture cannot be created (`WORKER_FIXTURE_MAX` in `src/runtime/worker.c`). Needs a contract decision if million-row stress is wanted.
3. `candidate.install` is synchronous; queue, boundary and publish cannot be split by the host. Needs an AGENT_RPC decision or an internal timing field.
4. Candidate slots are never evicted (16). Warm loops restart the process. Needs an eviction rule to measure long warm sessions.
5. T04 (7 of 14 negative cases) cannot be expressed through `capsule.submit`. Harness/contract owner decision.
6. M0-09 has no commit or handoff; M0-10 lists it as a dependency. Orchestrator to confirm status.
