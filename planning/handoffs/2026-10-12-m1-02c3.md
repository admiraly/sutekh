## 2. Design
- Ordering seam (test only). `su_agent_test` = the agent library compiled with `SU_TEST_FAULT_HOOKS` and
  `native_versions.c`. `SU_TEST_INTERLEAVE=candidate` makes the supervisor run `candidate.install` of the newest
  passed, never-installed candidate at the world's current revision, through `route()`, between admission and
  install; `close` runs `world.close`. One shot per process. The outer install then sees a new revision (or an
  unknown world) and is refused; the native record is cleared by the nested install, so the world reports tier `vm`.
- Own cases. `own_test_path` also accepts `test.json` beside `capsule.json` (t03). Admission corpus for a t03
  system: 42 corpus cases + 1 own case = 43 (checked for scalar and avx2 on all six fixtures, 259 checks total in
  test_specialize_admission).
- Own-case mismatch detail: `source: own_case`, `case`, `reason`, `field`, `row`, `expected`, `actual` (checked
  by the `wrong_world` scenario).
- TX4: `native_spawn_probe.py` runs `strace -f -e trace=execve,execveat` around a session (`engine.info`,
  `world.create`, `world.specialize` scalar and avx2, `capsule.submit`, `candidate.evaluate`, `candidate.install`,
  `world.specialize` again, `world.step`, `world.close`). Positive controls (`cc` via PATH and absolute path) must be
  detected first. Exit 0 pass, 1 build tool executed, 2 inconclusive, 3 not native (TX4 unavailable).

## 5. Deviations
1. Revision swaps: 6, not 1000. Each swap needs a candidate, and an agent process has 16 candidate slots. The 1000
   cycles are specialize-only (scalar/avx2 at one revision), which is the part the slot limit does not bound.
2. `world.close` during admission and stale revision are tested at the supervisor level through the seam; the
   seam is one-shot and in `su_agent_test` only (the production binary has no seam).
3. Layout generation change: tested at worker level (M2 red), not through a supervisor-level schema change.
4. The in-flight test covers candidate.install and world.close; a second world.specialize in flight is not
   reachable (the supervisor is synchronous).
5. No per-process in-flight counter is exposed to the test; "in-flight counter zero" is not observed directly.
6. Not done: the "retired artifact pointer still handed out" mutant; an RWX *final* mapping check beyond the
   churn mapping scan; clang sanitizer runs; default OFF Debug ctest.
7. TX3 has no self-test checks of its own (only TX4 pure checks were added).
8. ASan RSS bound needs `quarantine_size_mb=1` (documented in section 11).
