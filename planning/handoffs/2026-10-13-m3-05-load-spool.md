## Change
- `tests/rts/load/rts_session.py`: drives a real `sutekh serve`; builds the full six-system RTS world (contract 3.2
  capsule order: spatial host, goal, combat, move, scatter host, damage) from the committed capsules and an empty
  39-field fixture with `max_entities`. First end-to-end construction of the M3 world through the supervisor.
- `tests/rts/load/test_load_engine.py` (ctest `rts_load_engine`): T-RTS-LOAD-1 (a) near-maximum 1,024-row request
  (measured line length, one bump, 1,024 SPAWNED records in slot order, recovery after SIGKILL equals a control
  session, two ticks after), (b) 1,048,577 bytes refused with no change, (c) exactly 1,048,576 accepted and a bad last
  row at the cap is `SU_E_TYPE`, (g) capacity and domain refusals with revision, `world.inspect` and event log
  unchanged; spool precheck case.
- **Bug fix** `worker.c` `spawn_row_parse`: `formation_slot` was bounded by the fixture's row count (`inst.rows`), not
  by the entity capacity (`max(rows, max_entities)`), so every formation spawn into a world grown by `max_entities`
  was refused. Found by case (a). `set_fields` already used the column length (= capacity).
- Spool precheck of contract 12: the supervisor sends `spool_retain`/`spool_bytes_max` with admitted `world.spawn`
  and `world.spawn_batch`; the worker's `spawn_precheck` refuses when
  `(retain + 1) * (446 * hw_after + 90 * event_log_max) + 16384 > spool_bytes_max`. Recovery replays omit the keys.
- `docs/AGENT_RPC.md` section 18 updated; `tests/rts/load/PLAN.md` updated.
