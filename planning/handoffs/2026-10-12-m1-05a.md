## Probe coverage (`tests/platform/native_abi_probe.c`)

- Kernels: sub, add, mul, unsigned min, eq-select, unsigned max, const, and a 3000-register chain (frame over several pages, page-probe path). Backends: scalar always, AVX2 when `su_native_host_caps()` reports it.
- Row counts 0, 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9, 15, 16, 17, 33, 1000 (chain limited to 33). Columns are offset by one element so they are 4-byte but not 8-byte aligned, and guard words on both sides of each staging column catch out-of-range writes. Values come from a C reference, not the VM.
- Publication faults injected (`SU_NATIVE_FAIL_MMAP`, `SU_NATIVE_FAIL_MPROTECT`): each returns `SU_E_RESOURCE_LIMIT` with no artifact retained, then recovers. On Win64 the second exercises the `VirtualFree` cleanup.
- Win64 only: an assembly trampoline that follows the Win64 convention poisons rbx, rbp, rsi, rdi, r12-r15 and xmm6-15, calls the kernel with rcx, and compares after return. A negative control (a deliberately bad kernel clobbering rsi) is detected. The SysV clobber stays in `tests/native/scalar` (not portable).
- Not covered: fault recovery in a worker, a kernel fault (exception) on Win64, and anything on real Windows.

Owner: agent/worker. A Windows worker path is needed for C07 (see status doc).
2. **`tests/native/scalar/test_native_scalar.c` and `tests/native/avx2/test_native_avx2.c` are not portable** (`sys/mman.h`, `fork`, `mmap`). Not fixed; out of scope. The Win64 probe covers the Win64 kernel and publication paths instead.
3. **No Win64 unwind data** for JIT kernels or the probe's asm trampoline. Documented, not fixed. Consequence: an in-kernel exception cannot be unwound in process (see status doc).
4. **MSVC gets no AVX2 detection** (`su_native_caps.c` reports scalar only off GCC/Clang). Not a defect for GCC/Clang; MSVC is not built.
5. **No portability defect found in `src/platform/` or `backends/native/`**: no edits required. `su_platform.c`, `sutekh_main.c`, `su_native.c`, both AsmJit lowerings and `su_native_caps.c` compile clean for Win64 with strict flags, and their Win64 paths ran under wine.
6. `/proc/cpuinfo` on this VM does not list an `osxsave` flag, while CPUID leaf 1 reports OSXSAVE=1. The capability report keeps both; the CPUID/XGETBV reading is the one to trust.
