## Harness rules that are implemented
- Expected values come from `reference_eval.py` and are cross-checked against each fixture's own expected columns. A disagreement fails the test.
- Engine `ok`/`state` fields are never trusted alone. Error codes must match exactly. Negative cases also check that revision, tick and columns did not change.
- Statuses: `passed`, `failed`, `not_run`, `unavailable`. Unavailable tests report the real reply that caused it. A harness exception is `not_run` with the traceback, never `passed`.
- T00 runs `python3 tools/dev.py build` and checks `--version` against `include/sutekh/abi.h` and `ir.h`, not against the binary.
- T12 wraps the compiler tools on `PATH`, runs a control call to prove the wrapper logs, samples the process tree of the supervisor, and counts build invocations during the capsule-only swap window.
- T13 and T11 are `not_run` by design when no target or bound is declared (TEST_SPEC section 8 and the T11 requirement). Their measurements are recorded.
