## Deviations and conventions (all open for owner review)

1. **Numeric profile name**: `rts_u32_v1` follows the contract; the engine does not yet accept it (see above).
2. **Schema digest bytes**: 7.x/M2-01 section 6 names the sorted `(id, type, name, default)` list but not its byte form. Used canonical JSON (`sort_keys`, compact separators, ASCII) of that list. Value is a regression pin in `test_profile.py`.
3. **Canonical header newline**: 7.2 says "line 1 is canonical JSON". Used one LF after the JSON, then the row bytes; the digest covers that LF.
4. **Seeds in the lock** are JSON integers above 2^53 for some scenes. Python reads them exactly. Other parsers may not; a decimal string would be safer but would not match the unquoted `"seed"` in the canonical header.
5. **Member and remainder placement** (7.3 gives only team bands and the box): anchors, members and remainder rows draw positions from the team band (`mixed`) or the box (`congested`) independently of their anchor. Formation offsets are therefore not positional at tick 0. Draw order is recorded in the code.
6. **Team and order conventions**: `mixed` group team is a PRNG draw; `congested` teams interleave by group parity (groups) and slot parity (remainder); `sparse` team 1 with probability 1/8 (7.3 says "sparse teams" only); sparse orders are attack-move to the domain centre. Band-centre order target is `(lo + hi + 1) // 2`, y = 16384.
7. **Anchor speed**: anchors take the weapon speed of their group (anchors have range 0, so speed is the only weapon field that matters). 7.3 does not say.
8. **Weapon fields for members**: range, reload, damage, flight, speed and hp come from the 7.4 table. The domain check (2.2) passes for every row.
9. **Duel fixture (`duel16`)**: 16 hand-specified rows. Covers: exact-range pair (dist² = range_sq), x = 0 and x = 32767, equal-distance candidates, hp 3 target hit by two shooters, formation anchor and member. It does **not** encode the bow-flight-death case, the mid-run anchor despawn or the slot-reuse stale-projectile case: these need scheduled actions, not spawn rows. Their expected outcomes belong to M3-02 and M3-06. The exact expected outcomes of 10.2 are not in these rows.
10. **Validator**: `validate_scene` checks the 2.2 domains, the spawn idle values, the anchor idle values of 7.3, and the formation rules (slot is an anchor of the same team, gen nonzero iff slot set). `hp` upper bound 65535 is the proposed bound (OD-9).
11. **Byte bound**: a row with 39 values and `external_id` all at 4294967295 is 925 bytes (computed, and asserted in the tests). A 1,024-row array is at most 948,225 bytes. The emitter asserts this for every call.
